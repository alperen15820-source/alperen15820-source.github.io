Snake 3D — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 1, 2026

Snake 3D ("the app") is a casual game developed by Alperen Sezer. This policy explains what information the app handles.

Information we collect

The app itself does not require an account and does not collect, store or transmit any personal information. Your scores, settings and saved games are stored only on your device.

Advertising

The app shows ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and process certain device information (such as device identifiers, IP address, and coarse ad-interaction data) to serve and measure ads, subject to Google's own policies. You can learn more at Google's advertising policies and Google's Privacy Policy.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children. All game data stays on the device.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address with a new date.
