SNAKE CUBE 3D

Gold Edition · 2026

The classic snake — reborn in stunning 3D. Glide across a neon board, pass through portal walls, or crawl around all six faces of a rotating cube.

Game modes

  • Classic — walls are lethal, old-school reflex play
  • Infinite — portal walls wrap you to the other side
  • Cube — a true 3D twist: crawl across all 6 faces

Features

  • Three neon themes: Gold, Neon Pink and Emerald
  • 20 collectible snake skins earned by playing
  • Timed bonus food, three speed levels, auto-save
  • Haptics, sound effects and smooth 60fps 3D
